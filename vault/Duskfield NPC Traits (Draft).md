- They gradually forget. The brain becomes numb, and when they try to remember or recall anything their thoughts get interrupted. There's barely any stress in life so by trying to recall and think and stress oneself out, there's no purpose, since this is literally the only way one can be stressed out anymore, at least in Duskfield. You can pretty much do anything, life is simple. People are closely knit, in terms of support. There's no hidden meaning or content, people just feel and stay around each other. It's obvious though, however, that when one does try to speak, one cannot. Especially in the younger generations, they really have trouble, and at a certain point, it's not choice anymore, which actually makes it easier to maintain. For the parents, who were the ones who originally got exiled, it's a choice to remain silent, and so being voluntary in this process, it's not actually challenging. However, for the children, since they were born into the silence, though it's not voluntary, the nurture portion gradually outgrows the nature portion and though one is not deaf, one practically is. They are very good and proficient with music however, and express emotions through it, which is why the human portion of expression it is not completely supressed.
