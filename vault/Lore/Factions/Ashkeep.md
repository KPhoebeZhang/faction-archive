## Summary
#### Ashkeep — The Record-Keepers

Highly concentrated, snobbish, convinced the rest of civilization is illiterate and beneath them. Officially the keepers of history. In practice, their records are fragmented and contradictory by _clan_ — each family lineage holds a different version of history and distrusts the others' versions. This internal distrust prevents Ashkeep from ever producing a unified account, despite being the faction most invested in believing it has one.

Every five years, select Fornholt individuals are invited into the Ashkeep capital under the guise of formal education. In reality, Ashkeep elders secretly observe these students to see what they recall — because Fornholt's fractured memory occasionally contains fragments closer to the truth than Ashkeep's own curated records. Ashkeep needs Fornholt to maintain its sense of superiority, and won't admit it.
