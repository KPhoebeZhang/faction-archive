## Summary
#### Tidewall — The Traders

Wealthy, highly social, forbidden from marrying within the faction. Members must marry outsiders; if that outside partner (from Fornholt or Ashkeep) refuses to convert and join the trading life, both are exiled and become Duskfield, sent to a specific section of forest that other factions consider cursed.

Once a year, all Tidewall gather wearing masks that are never reused, ensuring members can never recognize each other afterward. During this ceremony, they exchange information and decide which sub-groups will control which trading routes for the year — then disperse and cannot maintain contact until the next gathering.

This system is a masterpiece of controlled connection: it allows information exchange and collective decision-making while guaranteeing no relationship, alliance, or shared understanding ever persists or compounds. Tidewall members are profoundly lonely and have normalized it. They are envious of the elder-youngling bonds they observe in Fornholt, and will sometimes intervene to protect Fornholt students from bullying in the Ashkeep capital, if they happen to like the individual.
