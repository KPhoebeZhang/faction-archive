## Summary
#### Fornholt — The Fractured

Generally considered inferior to Ashkeep. Carry the ancestral trauma of the collapse most directly — their memory is nonlinear, scratched, sometimes indistinguishable from fear. Outsiders read this as mysticism and "fortune telling." Some Fornholt elders genuinely can do something resembling fortune telling — but it isn't supernatural. Through long lives of learning to coexist with trauma they can't name or pinpoint, they've developed a cleared, intuitive sense of pattern — recognizing how past events shape present and future. Their "fortune" is assessment, not magic.

Some Fornholt resent Ashkeep deeply, seeing through their condescension and recognizing the relationship for what it is — extraction disguised as education.
