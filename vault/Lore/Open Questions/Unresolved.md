1. **The age-9 incident.** What happened, specifically — sensory detail, sequence, what she did or didn't do — that cost her brother's life, and which a single piece of recorded knowledge could have prevented. This is the emotional engine of the entire story and currently the most significant gap.
2. **The ages 13–15 gap.** What her relationship with her parents looked like during this period. Whether either parent revealed — even briefly — that they once had the same questions.
3. **The mechanism for her departure from Duskfield at 15.** How and why she begins traveling between factions — this is a plot mechanism as much as a character one, and likely connects to the broader history.
4. **Her voice.** What is specific, contradictory, or unmistakable about how she thinks and speaks — currently the protagonist is defined by what happened _to_ her rather than who she _is_.
5. **A living secondary relationship with real friction.** Someone she loves whose wants conflict with hers, whose safety or trust is genuinely at stake in her choices — currently absent. Without this, her choices carry intellectual weight but not interpersonal weight.
6. **The architects of the containment system.** Who they were, whether any trace of them or their reasoning still exists, and whether the system is still actively maintained by anyone in the present day — currently the system appears self-perpetuating through culture alone, but whether something or someone still tends it is unresolved.
7. **New Duskfield villages.** The explanation for how newly-exiled founders "already know how to run a village" despite no formal training — likely a thread back into the deeper history.
