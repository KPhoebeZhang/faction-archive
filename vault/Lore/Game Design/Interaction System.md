
### Layer 1 — Ambient/Passive (no UI, fully automatic)

Happens through physical presence and time. Player never consciously triggers these — they are consequences of existing in the world near NPCs.

- `Proximity` — being near an NPC affects their state subtly over time
- `Companionship` — cumulative time spent near a specific NPC builds relationship regardless of deliberate interaction. Most Rain World-like layer.
- `Linger` — staying still near someone for 4+ seconds triggers a warmth response
- `Receive` — if an NPC gives something, player takes it automatically. No button.

Player may not notice this layer working until an NPC behaves differently toward them.

### Layer 2 — Universal Deliberate UI (three buttons)

Same three buttons appear near every NPC regardless of faction. Context determines what each means per faction and per trust level.

`Offer` — one-directional outward. You give something.

- Duskfield: offering presence or stillness
- Ashkeep: offering a collected object or knowledge fragment
- Tidewall: offering a piece of player_knowledge in exchange
- Fornholt: offering a combination of knowledge cards (see Layer 3)

`Interact` — two-way exchange. Encompasses listen and converse.

- Low trust: mostly listening, NPC gives little
- High trust: opens into genuine exchange, deeper reveals
- Button is the same — the game decides what's possible based on state

`Observe` — attention turned inward or toward environment/NPC behavior.

- Triggers protagonist's internal thought box
- Used to examine objects, read a room, notice NPC behavior without engaging
- Language sophistication progression lives here
- In Duskfield early game: sparse physical thoughts, sometimes music instead of text
- Later in game: thoughts become more articulate as protagonist acquires faction vocabularies

### Layer 3 — Faction-Specific Mechanics (special UI)

Deeper designed interactions that make each faction mechanically distinct. Not available everywhere — unlocked by faction context and trust threshold.

#### Knowledge Synthesis UI (Fornholt + Ashkeep)

Two-panel drag-and-drop interface using collected knowledge cards:

- Left panel: player's knowledge cards (single words/short phrases from player_knowledge)
- Right panel: NPC's space
- Player drags 1-2 cards into NPC's panel
- Same mechanic, different emotional logic per faction:
    - Fornholt: NPC sees _pattern_ — connects fragments across time and trauma
    - Ashkeep: NPC sees _contradiction or confirmation_ — challenges or validates their records
- NPC response shaped by: faction value (coherence/certainty), trust, which cards offered
- Makes player_knowledge visible as physical draggable cards for first time
- Full-game feature — design now, build in later phase

### Language Sophistication Progression

Internal thought boxes evolve as protagonist travels and acquires faction vocabularies:

- Duskfield (start): sparse, physical, pre-verbal
    - "Sorted by feel. Same as always."
    - Sometimes music instead of text — pre-verbal emotional response
- After Ashkeep exposure: categorical, record-keeping vocabulary
- After Tidewall: relational, transactional vocabulary
- After Fornholt: pattern language, nonlinear connections
- Full sentences with faction-specific vocabulary by end of game

### Exploration Motivation

- Interactive objects trigger Observe → internal thought when examined
- Objects unlock topics in player_knowledge same as NPC interactions
- Knowledge cards from exploration become inputs for Layer 3 mechanics
- No quest markers, no blinking icons — rewards attentiveness
